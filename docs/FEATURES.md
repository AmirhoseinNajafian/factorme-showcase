# Feature map

This page describes the product at a user-facing level. It is not an API reference or a source-code specification.

## Workspace

- Personal dashboard with invoice and payment summaries.
- Account-specific business profile and settings.
- Navigation for dashboard, new invoice, saved invoices, account, and about.

## Invoice creation

- Customer name and contact details.
- Invoice title, number, and date.
- Service or product line items.
- Quantity, unit price, and row totals.
- Discount and tax inputs.
- Payment status and notes.
- Automatic subtotal, tax, and grand-total calculations.

## Invoice management

- Save, preview, edit, delete, and search invoices.
- Review previously saved invoices.
- Track payment state.
- Print and prepare output for customers.

## Invoice customization

- Business name, contact details, and logo.
- Brand and background colors.
- Multiple visual styles: Minimal, Classic, Bold, Modern, Soft, Elegant, and Corporate.
- Font selection and logo placement.
- Show or hide optional invoice sections.
- Live preview before producing the final document.

## Output and portability

- Browser print flow, including the browser's Save as PDF option.
- PNG and JPG image exports.
- Share actions supported by the browser/device.
- Account backup and legacy invoice import from browser storage or JSON export.

## Account experience

- Registration, sign-in, and sign-out.
- Business/work profile details.
- Account settings and invoice appearance settings.
- Account data separation as a core product requirement.

## Notes

Feature availability can differ between the local preview and hosting editions. Consult the instructions included with the relevant distribution package for its exact requirements and limitations.
