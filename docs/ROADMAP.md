# Roadmap

This roadmap describes product direction, not release commitments.

## Current focus

- Keep the Persian RTL experience consistent across all pages.
- Improve mobile layouts and touch-friendly invoice forms.
- Keep invoice customization predictable across presets and final output.
- Improve page transitions while preserving browser navigation behavior.
- Make legacy invoice import understandable and safe.

## Next areas to explore

- More invoice layouts for different business types.
- Saved customer and frequently used service/product lists.
- More flexible invoice numbering and business profile options.
- Additional export and print layout controls.
- Better onboarding and in-product help.

## Quality bar

New work should preserve account data separation, prevent accidental loss of form input, support keyboard and touch navigation, respect reduced-motion settings, and be checked at mobile and desktop widths before release.
